JARVIS 2.0

Privacy and Google data use

Prepared September 29, 2026 for a private owner-operated installation.

Information used

With the owner's Google authorization, JARVIS can access account identity and requested Gmail messages and metadata, calendars and events, Drive files and metadata, and contacts. Requested scopes may also allow Google document, spreadsheet and presentation operations. Availability and verification vary by workflow.

Purpose and processing

Data is used to answer the owner's requests and carry out authorized actions. Reasoning and speech use locally configured models. Google receives the service requests necessary for connected operations. A task that sends a message, shares a file or uses another external service can disclose the task data to its intended recipient or service; such actions remain subject to the owner's instructions and application authority.

Storage and retention

Google tokens are encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI for the Windows user. Conversations, tool results, generated files, action records and Obsidian notes can contain Google data. These local records are not all encrypted by the application and may persist until the owner removes them. Any separate backup or sync software configured by the owner may also copy local records.

Access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's Google access through Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents future authorized access; it does not automatically erase previously retained local records. The owner manages local records and any backups separately.

Google data commitments

Google user data is used for the owner's requested functionality, not advertising, sale of personal information, or training generalized AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Support

Contact the owner who operates and supplied this private installation for questions about its configured connections and stored data. The Google consent screen identifies the application's support contact.
